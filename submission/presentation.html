Why Test M3 Matters Scientifically

In Test M3 (5 mm thick, 2.2 cm wide, 1-sided PMMA), raising opposed airflow from 10 to 20 cm/s coincided with lower spread rate (0.029 → 0.024 mm/s) while test-level O2 dropped from 20.8% → 19.7% over 20.1 min.

Atlas Analytics Without Overclaiming
  • 20 tests ≠ 43 independent runs: Atlas tracks burns separately from stepped segments.
  • 12 / 13 assessable multi-velocity tests rise with airflow; M3 is flagged, never smoothed out.
  • M6 & M12 report "Not tracked" (4 segments kept null).
  • Each Table 5.1 row includes an inline Inspect button opening full provenance.
