# AegisμG — Evidence-First Competitive and Scientific Strategy

**Team:** SPECULATRON AI
**Challenge:** [2026 Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/)
**Release horizon:** November 14–15, 2026. Public deployment and official submission must be completed by the deadline, regardless of longer-term ambitions.

## 1. Challenge fit

The official challenge asks for an *interactive, super intelligence (SI) powered dashboard* that **summarizes, ranks, and interprets** NASA microgravity combustion findings for human space exploration. Relevant Space Apps judging dimensions described publicly include Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation. Do not assume old local-event scoring weights are necessarily applicable to the 2026 global process.

The defensible claim is *research discovery and scientific evidence triage*, NOT spacecraft fire-hazard certification.

## 2. What differentiates AegisμG

1. **NASA-first, not synthetic-first:** Complete Table 5.1 transcription from NASA/TM-20210011385, 20 unique PMMA tests and 39 numeric spread-rate observations. Maintain the separation of the 39 paired observations from 20 independent samples.
2. **What the data cannot tell us:** Preserve null values for M6/M12, per-segment oxygen and uncertainty; make missingness visible.
3. **Scientific exceptions and conflicts:** A 13-test directional analysis recognizes 12 aligned tests and the M3 exception rather than forcing a universal model. NASA's own report discusses model/measurement mismatch (FDS predictions higher than observed) without a derived numerical validation score.
4. **DIWA provenance:** Source identifiers, report page locators, explicit origin and verification status.
5. **SPECULATRON relevance intelligence:** Deterministic ranking with an inspectable formula, optional grounded AI that falls back safely when no API key is configured.
6. **Digital Twin Sandbox:** A transparent 0D hypothetical oxygen mass balance with no unsupported gravity scaling, separate from replay of actual NASA report values.
7. **Reproducibility:** Public code, source-preserving CSV export, tests and documented assumptions.

## 3. Benchmarks (public descriptions only, not validated overall rankings)

- [NASA Flame Intelligence](https://nasa-dash.vercel.app/) describes a large synthetic dataset and an un-deployed predictive model in its landing page.
- [FLARENEXUS](https://github.com/KhalidHasan03/Flame-in-Freefall) describes a feature-rich research interface with synthetic demonstration experiment records.
- [Fotia](https://github.com/AnteurAbderraouf/Fotia) emphasizes harmonizing fragmented NASA combustion data.

We should not copy these projects or assert superiority without empirical evaluation. The comparison suggests that **source authenticity, meaningful analysis of real records, limitation-aware reasoning, and reliable deployment** are useful differentiators.

## 4. High-priority acceptance evidence

- Live no-login public URL, mobile and desktop verified.
- NASA Table 5.1 screenshot/page citation and 20/20 test transcription reviewed.
- 39 source-reported numeric observations, four deliberately null spread values.
- A real inquiry: "Does faster ventilation always correspond to faster flame spread?" Answer shows the 12/13 pattern, M3 exception, and changing-oxygen caveat.
- A second inquiry: "Can these 0 g tests certify a Mars habitat?" Answer: No; no partial-gravity validation.
- A third inquiry: "Can the digital twin forecast fire risk?" Answer: No; it is a user-assumed oxygen inventory illustration.
- Code, tests, demo slides/video and AI-use disclosure publicly accessible.

## 5. Remaining advanced R&D — do not claim completed

- NASA PSI time-series collection with data-use rules and DOI attribution (NASA PSI Data Repository).
- Derived features with sample-specific oxygen time-series and actual instrument calibration.
- Real time-series reconciliation of temperature, flame-front position and ventilation history.
- Cross-validation with held-out NASA experiments, parameter uncertainty intervals, RMSE/MAE, conservation residuals and sensitivity plots.
- Physically verified reduced-order models, then FDS/CFD only with explicitly documented conditions and computation cost.
- Retrieval-augmented claim auditing with an exact primary citation and automated citation check, preserving human verification gates.
- Collaborative reproducible benchmark dataset and expert reviewer sign-off.
